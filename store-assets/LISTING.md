# YouListen - Chrome Web Store listing

## Name
YouListen

## Short description
Hide YouTube video, keep the audio and controls, and track listening versus watching time locally.

## Detailed description
Use YouTube as background audio without the moving picture pulling your attention away.

YouListen automatically hides video while keeping YouTube's audio and familiar playback controls. Browse, search, pick a video, and keep listening.

- Hide moving video by default with a quiet grey screen.
- Choose a static thumbnail on regular video pages.
- Reveal video until tomorrow, or resume hiding whenever you want.
- See listening and watching totals for 1W, 1M, or ALL recorded time.
- Keep preferences and statistics on your device, with no account required.
- Use a compact popup that follows your system's light or dark theme.

Listening includes hidden playback and playback outside the foreground. Watching requires revealed playback in your active tab and focused window. Pauses and buffering do not count; overlapping tabs count only once. Totals are approximate and are measured in elapsed time, not video duration.

YouListen uses plain JavaScript and CSS, with no runtime framework or developer-operated backend. Statistics are stored locally. Optional thumbnail mode requests the current video's image from YouTube's image service; see the privacy policy for details.

This extension hides the image; it does not reduce video downloads or guarantee CPU savings. It supports desktop www.youtube.com. Native picture-in-picture windows, YouTube Music, mobile sites, and embedded players on other websites are outside scope.

YouListen is open source under the MIT license. It is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google.
